Privacy Policy

Crovana Form House · Independent Journal of Interior Design & Architecture

Effective date: 1 January 2025 · Last updated: 15 March 2025

Crovana Form House respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it. We have written it in plain language because we believe privacy notices should be readable, not just legally sufficient.

1. Who We Are

Crovana Form House is an independent editorial journal covering interior design, residential architecture, materials, lighting, and space planning. Our website is crovanaformhouse.com.

For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar data protection laws, Crovana Form House is the data controller for personal information collected through this website.

If you have any questions about this policy or about how your data is handled, you can contact us at editor@crovanaformhouse.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect very little information, and only what is necessary to operate an editorial publication. Specifically:

2.1 Information you provide voluntarily

  • Newsletter subscription: If you subscribe to the Crovana Form House journal, we collect your email address. This is the only personal information required for the newsletter.
  • Email correspondence: If you contact us by email—for editorial enquiries, corrections, or story pitches—we receive your email address and any information you include in your message.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard technical information when you visit: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring page, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Essential cookies: We use only those cookies strictly necessary for the website to function. We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking cookies, or third-party analytics that build reader profiles.

We do not collect: names, postal addresses, phone numbers, payment information, government identifiers, or any special category data. We do not require account registration to read the journal.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used only for the following purposes:

  • To send the newsletter — if you have subscribed, we use your email address to deliver the journal. Every email includes a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • To respond to your messages — if you contact us, we use your email address to reply.
  • To understand readership — aggregated, anonymised server log data helps us see which articles are read and how readers find the site. This information is never used to identify individual readers.
  • To maintain the website — technical logs help us diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and keep the site secure.

We do not use your information for profiling, automated decision-making, or advertising. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to anyone.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the GDPR, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): For newsletter subscriptions. You provide consent when you enter your email address and confirm your subscription. You may withdraw consent at any time by unsubscribing or by contacting us.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): For server logging, website security, and responding to correspondence. We have a legitimate interest in operating a secure, functional publication and in communicating with our readers.
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)): Where we are required to retain or disclose information to comply with applicable law.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Crovana Form House uses only essential cookies — small text files placed on your device that are strictly necessary for the website to function. We do not use cookies for advertising, profiling, or cross-site tracking.

Cookie Purpose Duration
session_id Maintains your session while browsing the site. Essential for basic functionality. Session
cookie_consent Records that you have seen the cookie notice so it is not shown repeatedly. 12 months

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may affect how the website works. For more detail, please see our Cookie Notice.

6. Third-Party Services

We keep third-party services to an absolute minimum. Currently:

  • Newsletter delivery: If you subscribe, your email address is stored with our email service provider solely for the purpose of sending the journal. They are contractually prohibited from using your data for any other purpose.
  • Web hosting: Our hosting provider processes server logs on our behalf. They act as a data processor under a data processing agreement.
  • Embedded images: Some articles include images hosted on external services. Loading these images may transmit your IP address to those services as a normal part of internet communication.

We do not embed third-party analytics scripts, advertising networks, social media trackers, or behavioural profiling tools.

7. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected:

  • Newsletter email addresses: Retained until you unsubscribe, after which they are deleted from our active list. Suppression records (to ensure we do not re-add you) may be kept for a limited period.
  • Correspondence: Emails are kept for as long as needed to handle the enquiry, and then for a reasonable period for reference, after which they are deleted.
  • Server logs: Typically retained for a maximum of 90 days, after which they are automatically deleted or anonymised.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of access: You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: You can ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to erasure: You can ask us to delete your personal information, subject to certain legal exceptions.
  • Right to restrict processing: You can ask us to limit how we use your information.
  • Right to data portability: You can request your information in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Right to object: You can object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at editor@crovanaformhouse.com. We will respond within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

9. International Data Transfers

Crovana Form House is operated from within the European Economic Area (EEA). Where we use service providers located outside the EEA—for example, an email delivery service based in the United States—we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

If you are reading this journal from outside the EEA, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the EEA or in the location of our service providers.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the small amount of personal information we hold. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls on our systems, and using reputable service providers with strong security practices.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a data breach affecting your rights, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

11. Children's Privacy

Crovana Form House is an editorial journal intended for a general adult readership. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in applicable law. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify newsletter subscribers by email.

We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of the website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, or to exercise your data protection rights, please contact:

Crovana Form House
Editorial & Data Enquiries
editor@crovanaformhouse.com

We aim to respond to all privacy-related enquiries within 30 days.
