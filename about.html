About the Journal

Form, Light, and the Logic of Home

Why we write about architecture and interiors the way we do

Crovana Form House is an independent editorial journal about interior design and architecture. It is written for people who want to understand not just what looks good, but why certain spaces work — and why others, despite appearances, do not.

We publish long-form articles, practical guides, and case studies. Our editorial position is simple: form follows function, materials should be honest, light matters more than color, and the best interiors are the ones that are easiest to live in. We are not affiliated with any architectural practice, manufacturer, or retailer, and we accept no sponsored content. Every article is written because we believe the subject deserves attention, not because someone paid for it to be covered.

What We Look For

We are drawn to projects that solve real problems — how to bring light into a deep plan, how to make a small apartment feel generous, how to build with materials that improve as they age. We are less interested in spectacle than in coherence. A house does not need to be large or expensive to be worth studying. It needs to be thoughtful.

Our case studies often focus on a single decision — a window, a stair, a material choice — and trace how that decision shapes the experience of the whole building. We believe that understanding architecture is a skill anyone can develop, and that it starts with learning to read the spaces you already inhabit.

“The best interiors are not the ones that photograph well. They are the ones that feel inevitable — where every element seems to have been placed by someone who understood how the room would actually be used.”

Editorial Values

  • 01

    Honest materials

    We favour surfaces that are what they appear to be — real wood, real stone, real concrete — over imitations that age poorly and mislead the eye.

  • 02

    Light before color

    No paint colour can compensate for insufficient daylight. We treat natural light as the primary material of every interior.

  • 03

    Circulation as comfort

    A beautiful room you have to walk through to reach the kitchen is a corridor with furniture. We pay attention to how spaces connect, not just how they look in isolation.

  • 04

    Negative space

    A room filled with furniture has stopped being designed. We value the empty areas that give the objects around them room to be seen.

Why We Write

Most design publishing falls into two camps: glossy showcases that celebrate expense, or technical manuals that assume professional training. Crovana Form House exists in the space between. We write for the reader who is designing a home, renovating a flat, or simply trying to understand why one room feels better than another.

Our articles are not reviews and not advertisements. They are attempts to think clearly about the built environment, in public, with the reader as a collaborator in the process. If you finish an article with a new way of seeing a space you thought you already knew, we have done our job.
