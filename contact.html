Contact the Journal

Editorial enquiries · Story pitches · Corrections & correspondence

Get in touch

Write to Crovana Form House

We welcome considered correspondence: story pitches that fit the journal’s editorial focus, corrections, and questions about published material. We read every message, though we cannot always reply to unsolicited pitches.

Send a message

Please be concise. For pitches, 2–3 paragraphs is plenty. Links to published work are welcome.

Stay with the Journal

One email per week. Long-form articles, practical guides, and new case studies—no noise, no sales pitches.

Editorial Office

Email

editor@crovanaformhouse.com

For all editorial enquiries, pitches, and corrections.

Response Time

We aim to reply to editorial correspondence within 5–10 working days. Pitch decisions are usually communicated within two weeks. If you do not hear back, please assume your pitch was not a fit for the current editorial calendar.

What We Publish

Crovana Form House publishes long-form essays, practical guides, and case studies on:

  • — Residential architecture
  • — Interior design principles
  • — Materials & finishes
  • — Lighting design
  • — Space planning
  • — Modern homes & adaptive reuse

We do not publish sponsored content, advertorials, or product promotions.

About the Editor

Elena Marsh is a writer and editor covering residential architecture and interior design. She has worked on editorial teams at design publications in London and Copenhagen.
