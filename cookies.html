Cookies Policy

Crovana Form House · Independent Journal of Interior Design & Architecture

Effective date: 1 January 2025 · Last updated: 15 March 2025

This Cookies Policy explains how Crovana Form House uses cookies and similar technologies on our website. We keep our use of cookies minimal and transparent — only what is strictly necessary to deliver a functional, secure reading experience.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, tablet, or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to the site owners.

Cookies can be "session cookies" (which are deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (which remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them). They can also be "first-party cookies" (set by the website you are visiting) or "third-party cookies" (set by a domain other than the one you are visiting).

Similar technologies include web beacons, pixels, and local storage. This policy covers all of these technologies, though Crovana Form House currently uses only standard HTTP cookies.

2. How We Use Cookies

Crovana Form House uses cookies for one purpose only: to make our website function correctly. We do not use cookies for advertising, behavioural targeting, cross-site tracking, or analytics that build reader profiles.

Specifically, cookies help us:

  • Maintain your session — so that pages load correctly and your browsing experience is consistent.
  • Remember your cookie preference — so that you are not shown the cookie notice on every visit.
  • Protect the website — basic security cookies help prevent cross-site request forgery and other common web attacks.

We do not use: advertising cookies, social media tracking pixels, cross-site behavioural tracking, or any third-party analytics that identify individual readers.

3. Cookies We Use

The table below lists the specific cookies that may be set when you visit Crovana Form House. All of them are strictly necessary for the website to operate.

Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
session_id Essential · First-party Maintains your browsing session. Without this, the website cannot function reliably. Session
cookie_consent Essential · First-party Records that you have seen the cookie notice so it is not shown repeatedly. 12 months
csrf_token Essential · First-party Helps protect against cross-site request forgery attacks on forms. Session

These cookies do not collect personal information, do not track you across other websites, and are not shared with any third party for marketing or advertising purposes.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Crovana Form House does not intentionally set third-party cookies. However, some content embedded in our articles — such as images hosted on external services — may cause those services to set their own cookies or receive your IP address as a normal part of internet communication.

We have no control over the cookies set by external services. If you would like to understand how those services handle data, we recommend reviewing their respective privacy and cookie policies.

If we ever introduce a third-party service that sets cookies, we will update this policy and, where required by law, seek your consent before any non-essential cookies are placed.

5. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can manage your cookie preferences in several ways:

5.1 Browser settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically:

  • View what cookies are stored and delete them individually.
  • Block third-party cookies.
  • Block cookies from specific sites.
  • Block all cookies from being set.
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser.

Please note that if you block or delete essential cookies, some parts of the Crovana Form House website may not function correctly. For guidance on managing cookies in your specific browser, please refer to your browser's help documentation.

5.2 Opt-out tools

Because we do not use advertising or analytics cookies, there is nothing to opt out of beyond the essential cookies described above. If you wish to avoid our essential cookies entirely, you can browse the site with cookies disabled, though functionality may be affected.

Your choice matters: We respect your decision to block cookies. If you disable essential cookies, the website will still be readable, but certain interactive features may not work as intended.

6. Your Consent

When you first visit Crovana Form House, you will see a brief cookie notice explaining that we use only essential cookies. Because these cookies are strictly necessary for the website to function, they do not require your prior consent under applicable law (including the EU ePrivacy Directive).

By continuing to use our website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Cookies Policy. If you do not agree with our use of essential cookies, please adjust your browser settings accordingly or discontinue use of the site.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.

If we ever introduce new categories of cookies (for example, analytics or advertising), we will update this policy and, where required, request your consent before placing those cookies.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.

For more details about how we handle personal information generally, please see our Privacy Policy.
